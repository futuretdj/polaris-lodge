Polaris

An aurora hotel in Inari, Finnish Lapland. Twelve rooms with glass roofs at the edge of the tree line. We switch off the lights at nine. The sky does the rest. See the rooms →

scroll into the night

Most hotels sell you a room. We sell you the four hours between ten and two, when the thermometer drops past minus twenty, the forest goes silent, and the sky starts to move. You will not need an alarm. Someone knocks on your door when it begins.
